How to build the V

  • Wide top: lats (Mon, Fri) and side delts (Tue, Sat) get the most weekly sets.
  • Legs: Thursday is cardio and core with only light leg work, to keep the legs the size they are.
  • Narrow waist: train abs, but skip heavy weighted side bends. Keep body fat in check with diet.
  • Progressive overload: log the dumbbell weight per exercise. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, go up 1–2 kg.
  • Tempo: 2 s down, 1 s up. Leave 1–2 reps in reserve on most sets.
  • Ticks reset every Monday. Saved weights stay.
